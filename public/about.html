The Realm of ELA

About This Platform

The Realm of ELA is a classroom learning platform for 5th grade English Language Arts at Lake Charles Charter Academy. It delivers the school's adopted Ready Magnet curriculum through a self-paced fantasy game structure, letting students move through lessons at their own pace while the teacher tracks where each student is.

Students travel through six lands, each aligned to a curriculum unit and its Louisiana ELA standards. Completing lessons earns experience points and in-game currency; each unit ends with a skill checkpoint. The structure is designed to support motivation and self-direction during independent work.

The platform is built and operated by the classroom teacher for use with her own students. It is not a commercial product, is not offered outside this classroom, and contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking. It stores no student-identifying information. Students are represented by character names they choose themselves.

For network administrators: this site connects to Google Firebase for data storage, including a WebSocket connection to *.firebaseio.com. Full technical details are available on request.
